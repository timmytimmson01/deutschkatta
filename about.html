Mrs. Solapurkar graduated with a BA in English from Pune University. She developed a love for the German language after visiting her son in Zürich, Switzerland. When she returned to India, she decided to pursue it further. She attained a Zertifikat, Diploma, and Advanced Diploma from the Randhe Institute. Moreover, has A2–B2 qualifications. She started teaching German in a private institute in 2013. Her teaching style is tailored to fit each student’s learning style and age group, ensuring they feel supported throughout their German studies, thereby instilling a love of the German language and a heightened confidence.
