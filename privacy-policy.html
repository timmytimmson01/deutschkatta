Privacy Policy

Deutsch Katta values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our website and contact form.

Information we collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message or reason for contacting us
  • Any other information you choose to share in the enquiry form

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Provide information about German language courses
  • Support your learning and enrollment questions

Third-party services

Our contact form may send submitted information to a third-party form processing service, such as Web3Forms, so that messages can be received and managed. These providers process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and security measures.

Data protection

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Retention

We keep enquiry information only for as long as necessary to respond to your request, manage communication, or comply with legal obligations.

Your rights

If you would like to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, please contact us using the information available on this website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the contact form or by using the contact details listed on the website.
